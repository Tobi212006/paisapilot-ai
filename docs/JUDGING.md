# Three-minute judge walkthrough

1. Open the live demo linked in the README. Choose Open workspace and stay in the sample workspace. No real statement or login is necessary.
2. In Overview, select the current month. Inspect totals and their evidence: recorded income/expenses, refunds and exclusions.
3. Open Transactions. Use the sample import/CSV review workflow; inspect proposed records and duplicate warnings before confirming. Show undo and restore. Use synthetic records only.
4. In Budget, save a category limit below recorded spending to demonstrate a visible overspending result. In Goals, create a small sample goal and add a contribution.
5. In UPI Safety, inspect a sample suspicious payment message and its matched risk signals. Explain that a warning is educational and cannot confirm fraud.
6. Show Currency and its dated reference-rate source. Open Credit Coach for transparent credit arithmetic. Collapse the sidebar or show the mobile layout; pause motion.
7. Explain Pip's role: optional plain-language education on top of visible calculations. Do not promise a live AI response: final hosted verification remains pending. All core recordkeeping and arithmetic work without a provider key.

## Hackathon theme fit

The core direction is smart budgeting: reviewed financial records become monthly summaries, budgets and explainable evidence. Supporting education covers payment safety, credit, fund data and illustrative business arithmetic. These are selected directions from the theme, not a claim to implement every suggested category. No real-money trading is present.

## Evaluation boundaries

Use README and STATUS.md to distinguish delivered functions from pending acceptance checks. Do not present sample transactions as bank-synchronized data, heuristic alerts as proven fraud, D1 contract tests as live provider tests, or an AI-generated sentence as a verified financial recommendation.
